Shell, frame and slots

D2 shell/Shell.dc.html

The pages mount the D2 shell: plant routes under /$plant/core/users and /$plant/core/roles, with the entries core.users and core.roles in Administration. Decision of 2026-10-08 on #313: administration moves into one Settings area with a secondary sidebar, so the frame around these pages changes once #313 has a direction; the content does not. Page actions render in TopBarActions (New user, New role, Edit role, Reset password, Block user; at most one default Button, folded into Page actions at 320). The header slot beside the h1 holds the StatusBadge and the username on a user, and the kind of a role on a role. These pages add no module slot.
