Shift colours on the calendar's week view

#770 · design notes for the build · every value is a proposal until Krister picks a variation

  • The colour follows the shift by its order in the calendar version: the first shift takes shift-1, the second shift-2, the third shift-3. A calendar with one shift looks almost as today. A fourth shift starts over at shift-1; its name stays written.
  • Each block keeps the shift's name and times, and the legend above the view names every shift, so colour is never the only cue (WCAG 1.4.1).
  • Break stays muted and dashed, overtime stays the success pair, non-working stays hatched destructive, and closed time has no fill. The order palette and the equipment group colours stay unused.
  • Ratios below are WCAG 2.x, measured in sRGB as tokens.contrast.test.ts measures them. The week view draws on card; lane and lane-alt are listed for the board.

Why new tokens

  • info, warning, success and destructive already mean planned, warning, overtime and non-working; three shifts would borrow two meanings.
  • palette-1 to palette-20 group orders on the board and group-1, group-2 mark equipment groups; a shift in an order colour would read as an order.
  • The build adds the pairs to apps/web/src/styles/tokens.css and their pairs to tokens.contrast.test.ts, and the design system republishes its tokens (ADR 0079).
