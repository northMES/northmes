Settings area with a secondary sidebar: variations

Issue #313 asks for a variations round before the spec page, because the settings area is a new kind of screen. Krister Johansson decided on 2026-10-08 that one Settings area with its own secondary sidebar holds the administration, grouped by scope, and replaces both the /admin mount and the Administration section at the foot of the plant sidebar; master data used in daily work stays in the main sidebar. This page draws three whole options: A, its own sidebar; B, the rail and a scope picker; C, in the page. Each draws Users at Acme AB and Machines at Plant A at 1440 in light, a plant admin who administers Plant A only and the way in from Plant A's board, both in dark, and the 320 reflow; B and C add a second 320 frame for the control their reflow adds. The rest of the issue's frame list (1280 and 1920, empty, onboarding and no-plant states, long strings, keyboard and focus) follows on the spec page for the chosen option.
