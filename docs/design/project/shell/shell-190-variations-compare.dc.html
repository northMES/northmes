Recommendation: A with the plant crumb and status split from C, and S1

A is the layout P06 describes and shadcn Sidebar already builds, every module's top-level entries are one click from the board, and long labels can wrap in the sidebar instead of overflowing a bar. Its weak points sit in the rail: the plant name disappears and the groups need monograms. Taking C's plant switcher as the first breadcrumb crumb keeps the plant visible in every sidebar state and at 320 px (the shell renders it in front of PageFrame's crumbs; V4). The switcher leaves A's sidebar head, which keeps the mark and the collapse button, so the page has one plant switcher. At 320 px the crumb needs the room C3 frees, so help, the account and the assistant move into the navigation sheet as in C4. C's split of the status (a chip for reconnecting, a strip under the top bar for the readiness, budget and restore banners) gives every message a place that never covers focus, as long as scroll-padding-top includes the strip. The monogram rail stays until the icon question (V1) is decided. B gives the board the most width, but it costs a second header row, a second click across modules, a tab order that reaches the account before the module entries and a change to the P06 text, and it has no collapsed rail for the frame list.

For the station, S1. The Windows touch keyboard opens at the bottom edge and would cover S2's bar, and so would the app keypad if D4 docks it there. P09 section 9 also gives the station a top bar for "Live updates paused, reconnecting", and S1 reuses the planner header. S3 gives the heading the most weight, but it puts the h1 outside main and takes 96 px. S2 deserves a second look if pilot IT chooses a portrait screen (IT-22).
