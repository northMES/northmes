Recommendation: A, its own sidebar, with B's scope picker and B's routes

A is the decision of 2026-10-08 as written: one Settings area with its own sidebar, reached from the sidebar foot where admins already found Administration, left through a Back control that names the plant, with the table at the width #222 chose and one navigation landmark at a time; at 320 it reuses the D2 navigation sheet, as the issue asks. Two parts of A do not hold up, and B answers both better. A's one tree moves plant pages out of /$plant, which breaks the rule of ADR 0007 that the plant rides in the route and with it the plant gate, the per-plant client and the Assistant; B keeps plant settings at /$plant/settings/... and puts company settings at /settings/$companyId/..., the mount that replaces /admin. A's grouped list grows with every company and plant and leaves module settings without a scope; B's picker shows one scope's entries, module settings included, and turns into a static head for a user with one scope. The recommendation is therefore A's sidebar, way in and sheet, with B's scope picker at the sidebar head, the Back control moved to the sidebar foot where the Settings link sits in the main sidebar, and B's route pattern, at the cost of a possible full page load when the picker moves between company and plant scope. C is the cheapest to build but splits Settings into two areas and leaves the table at about 730 px at 1280.
