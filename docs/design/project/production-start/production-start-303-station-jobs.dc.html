Station home: build notes for JO1 to JO25 and JO27 to JO31

The implementer takes the layout, the region order, the states and transitions, the copy in quotes verbatim, the keyboard model and the slot placements; never the markup, class names, token values, canvas icons or demo numbers. Every person, job, article and machine on these frames is fictional.

Layout and frame

  • The D2 station frame through the byte-equal copy production-start/StationFrame.dc.html, with the props size, theme, connection and station. The frame owns the top bar, the h1 "Press 4, Anna Berg", the connection chip, the Disconnected strip and the idle warning; the station screen adds no second top bar.
  • Route /station/$stationId, document title "Press 4 · Plant A · NorthMES".
  • 1920 by 1080: the job list 420 px, the job column, the last reports 600 px, gaps of 20 px. 1280 by 800: the job list 320 px and the job column, which scrolls, with the last reports under the report card. Portrait 800 by 1280: the Change job bar over the job column, which scrolls. These widths are proposals (rules 19.1 to 19.3).
  • At a station with several machines the machine switch sits in the frame's header slot beside the h1 (JO22, JO30, JO31). Release 1 draws nothing for the later slot production-start/station/panels/v1.

Components by their shadcn names, and tokens

  • Card: the list column, the job header, the report card, the last reports and "No job selected".
  • Button: each job row is one button at least 96 px high; Start, Pause and Finish are large outline buttons (48 px) in fixed slots; Send is the default button, at least 200 px wide; Try again and "Show jobs on Press 3" are large outline buttons.
  • Badge: StatusBadge with icon and word (planned Info, active Play, paused Pause, finished CircleCheck); the flags "Start overdue" and "Planned on Press 3"; "Unsent entries" with Upload.
  • Scroll Area for the job list and for the job column at 1280 and in portrait; Skeleton while loading; Alert for the job list error and the stale list; Sheet for Change job in portrait (JO27, JO28); Table for the last reports; Toggle Group for the machine switch.
  • D1 tokens only: the status pairs, --destructive and --destructive-subtle for errors, --warning and --warning-subtle for the stale list, --accent for the selected row and the skeletons, --border, --card and --muted-foreground. Targets follow --nm-target-min-station (44 px); 48 px buttons and 96 px rows are proposals (question 21.9).

Job list

  • The job orders on the station's equipment sorted by planned start, plus every job order with an open run on that equipment whatever its planned machine: 5006.10 shows "Planned on Press 3". Priority shows as "Priority 2" and is not a sort key (question 21.16). Jobs finished today follow under "Finished today" (question 21.2).
  • The list is fetched at sign-in, read cache-first and kept live by the station subscription. Remaining is quantity minus good minus scrap, printed with formatQuantity in the stock unit ("548 of 1 200 pcs left"); times are plant time.
  • The selected row has aria-current="true", a 2 px foreground border, the accent fill and a ChevronRight. Choosing a row moves focus to the job heading, an h2 with tabindex="-1" (JO6, JO29).
  • Portrait: Change job (aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded) opens the Sheet "Jobs on Press 4" from the right edge, 560 px wide, modal, with Close. Opening moves focus to the selected row (JO27, JO28); choosing a row closes the Sheet and moves focus to the job heading (JO29); Escape and Close return focus to Change job.
  • Machine switch: one 48 px outline button per machine in the group "Machine", the shown machine with aria-pressed="true". After a switch the list, the job and the last reports follow the machine and focus moves to the list heading "Jobs on Press 3" (JO30, JO31).

Start, Pause, Finish and Sending

  • Start moves planned or paused to active, Pause moves active to paused, Finish moves active or paused to finished; each is idempotent by target state. Each command keeps its own slot, and a command the status does not allow leaves its slot empty, so the second tap of a gloved double press lands on nothing (JO6 to JO8).
  • Every station mutation runs with AbortSignal.timeout(15000) and no optimistic response: the StatusBadge, the figures and the list row keep their values until the server answers (JO9 to JO13).
  • While it waits, the pressed button reads "Sending" with LoaderCircle, has aria-disabled="true" and aria-busy="true" and keeps focus, and a visually hidden role=status says "Sending". Send does the same for a report. The client idle timer does not fire while a command is in flight.
  • Send is aria-disabled="true" while Start, Pause, Finish or Send waits (ADR 0033, plan 09 section 7): it keeps its label and its place, stays focusable and shows the D1 disabled look (JO9 to JO11).
  • When the answer removes the pressed button from its slot, focus moves to the job heading and the polite region says "5001.20 paused" ("started", "finished").
  • After 15 s without an answer the error sits under the commands or under Send, the pressed button names it with aria-describedby, focus stays on that button and announce() says it once in the polite region (JO14 to JO17): "No answer from NorthMES within 15 seconds. Press Pause again; a job that already paused stays paused." and "No answer from NorthMES within 15 seconds. Your entries are kept. Press Send again; the report is counted once." The entries and the client_report_id stay, so the report counts once.

Page states

  • Loading: four skeleton rows in the list and skeletons of the job header, the report card and the last reports. Each region carries aria-busy="true", because the frame has no busy prop for main (question 21.32).
  • Empty: "No jobs on Press 4" with "Jobs planned on Press 4 show here by planned start."; at a station with several machines also "Show jobs on Press 3". The operator cannot create a job, so the state has no create action.
  • Error with nothing cached: "Jobs could not be loaded." with "Error id 7f3a91c2." and Try again. List not refreshed: "The job list could not be refreshed. It shows the jobs as of 13:40. Error id 7f3a91c2." over the cached rows with Try again; the selected job and the report form keep working (JO25).
  • With no job to show, the job area holds the h2 "No job selected" and one line: "Choose a job in the list when one is planned on Press 4." (empty) or "Choose a job in the list once it has loaded." (error).

Keyboard, focus and announcements

  • Tab order: Skip to main content, Switch operator, Sign out, More, the machine buttons when shown, the job rows in list order (Change job in portrait), the commands, the report fields, the keypad toggle, the scrap reasons as one stop, Send, the Correct buttons. Keypad keys stay out of the tab order.
  • Focus after choosing a job and after a command that removes the pressed button: the job heading. After Try again succeeds the button is gone, so focus moves to the list heading "Jobs on Press 4". After a machine switch: the list heading "Jobs on Press 3".
  • When a message pushes the focused control out of the job column's view, the column scrolls it back into view (JO17).
  • Polite region: "Sending", each inline error once, "5001.20 paused". A lost connection speaks only through the frame's Disconnected strip.
