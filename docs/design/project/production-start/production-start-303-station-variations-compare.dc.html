Recommendation: A, list beside the job, with the docked keypad from B

A keeps the most in view for the least new code. The operator sees every job on the machine, the selected job and its form at once, so changing job is one tap, the job with unsent entries carries an Unsent entries badge in the list, and the last reports sit beside the form at 1920 and one scroll below it at 1280. Status stays the StatusBadge that every other NorthMES screen uses, Sending shows on the pressed button with a polite status, errors appear under it, and corrected totals in a Dialog need no minus key. Its weak spot is quantity entry on a touch-only station, where the system keyboard covers half the screen. B's docked keypad covers that, so the spec page draws A with the keypad docked beside the fields. On a station without a keyboard the keypad shows by default, and the docked keypad keeps B's System keyboard toggle; on a station with a keyboard the keypad starts hidden and the Keypad toggle shows it (Q3). B's other parts cost context, since the list and the last reports hide behind Change job and a tab. C's tiles read well from a distance but put a screen between every two reports; its steppers could come back as 1 and 10 keys on the keypad if the glove test shows that small counts are common.
