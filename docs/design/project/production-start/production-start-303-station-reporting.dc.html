Reporting and corrections: build notes for RE1 to RE30

The implementer takes the layout, the region order, the states and transitions, the copy in quotes verbatim, the keyboard and keypad model and the focus targets; never the markup, class names, token values, canvas icons or demo numbers. Every person, job, article, machine and number on these frames is fictional.

Frames and parts

  • Every frame mounts the D2 station frame through the byte-equal copy production-start/StationFrame.dc.html, with the job list, the job, the report form and the last reports in option A's layout and option B's docked keypad (decision of 2026-10-08, rules D.1 to D.4).
  • The dialogs mount over the frame's overlay and are centered on the station screen; the station content keeps its layout under the overlay (rule 19.25).
  • RE1 to RE5: the Alert Dialogs for a large quantity and for the same report within 10 minutes. RE6 to RE15: the correction Dialog in its states, with the docked keypad on a station without a keyboard and behind Keypad on a station with one. RE16 and RE17: the last reports after a correction. RE18 and RE19: reports from an earlier production day.
  • RE20 to RE26: the report form's own states: the searchable list of 14 reasons, a quantity in kg with the decimal key at a station with two machines, a server error that keeps the entries, and the field errors with the summary. RE27 to RE30: the keypad toggles on both kinds of station.

Components by their shadcn names, and tokens

  • Alert Dialog: "Report 900 good?" and "Report again?", each with the safe choice first and focused. Dialog: the correction, 900 px wide with the keypad and 640 px without it, with Close as a 48 px ghost IconButton.
  • Field, Label and NumberField (QuantityInput) for Good quantity and Scrap quantity, 56 px; Textarea for Reason (required); Command for the searchable reasons with its search field and option list; Radio Group for eight reasons or fewer; Alert for the refusal; Table for the last reports; Toggle for System keyboard and Keypad; Toggle Group for the machine switch.
  • D1 tokens only: --destructive and --destructive-subtle for the refusal, the error summary and the field errors; --muted for the original report and the correction row; --accent for the active option and a pressed key; the D1 disabled look for an aria-disabled button; the D1 focus ring.

Large quantity and Report again

  • Large quantity: good plus scrap above the remaining quantity times the plant setting (default 1.5) asks first, before anything is sent. On 5001.20 the limit is 822 pcs. "Report 900 good" sends with confirmedLargeQuantity; "Change quantity" closes the dialog and moves focus to Good quantity with the entries kept.
  • Report again: the same job and quantities within 10 minutes under a new client_report_id asks first. "Report again" sends with confirmedRepeat; "Do not report" closes the dialog, keeps the entries and returns focus to Send. The 13:58 report is in view in the last reports at 1920 (RE4).
  • Both are role=alertdialog, modal, named by the title and described by the text. Escape is the safe choice. After a confirmed send, focus stays on Send and the line under it says what was reported.

Correcting a report

  • Correct on a quantity report of the current production day opens the Dialog "Correct the report of 13:58", named by its title and described by "The report stays as it is. Saving adds a correction row that refers to it." Focus goes to Good quantity.
  • The fields hold the corrected totals, so no minus sign is typed; "Change: -2 good, scrap unchanged" is computed under them and is part of the reading order. Reason (required) is free text.
  • Save correction with no reason: focus moves to Reason, which gets aria-invalid="true" and is described by "Enter a reason for the correction." (RE9, RE10).
  • While it waits, Save correction reads "Sending" with aria-disabled and aria-busy and keeps focus; a hidden status says "Sending" (RE13, RE14). A refusal (CORRECTION_EXCEEDS_ORIGINAL after another correction landed first) shows the Alert above the footer, keeps the values, and Save correction is described by it and keeps focus (RE11, RE12).
  • After a saved correction the Dialog closes, focus returns to that row's Correct, the polite region says "Correction of 13:58 saved", the correction row leads the last reports, and the job's figures and list row show the new totals: Good 638, Remaining 550 pcs, last report 14:06 (RE16, RE17). Cancel, Close and Escape also return focus to Correct.
  • A report from an earlier production day shows "Needs a supervisor" in place of Correct, with the note under the table (RE18, RE19; question 21.6).

Keypad and toggles

  • Station without a keyboard: the keypad is docked beside the fields (beside the reasons in portrait) with System keyboard in its caption row, aria-pressed="false". Pressing it hides the keypad, gives the field inputmode="numeric" (decimal for kg) and returns focus to the field; the pressed toggle then ends the fields row (RE29).
  • Station with a keyboard: the keypad starts hidden and Keypad ends the fields row, aria-pressed="false"; the reasons take two columns at 1920 and in portrait and four at 1280 (RE27, RE28). Pressing Keypad docks it, and the pressed toggle moves into the keypad's caption row with focus on it (RE30). The station keeps the choice in localStorage.
  • The correction Dialog docks the keypad the same way; with the keypad hidden, the toggle starts the footer (RE15). The keypad cannot type the reason (question 21.34).
  • A unit with display decimals adds the key "," named "Decimal comma"; Clear moves to the caption row beside the toggle, so the keypad keeps four rows (RE22, RE23).

Report form states

  • More than eight reasons: a Command with "Search scrap reasons", the count "2 of 14 reasons" under it and the option list; the chosen reason has Check and aria-selected="true"; arrow keys move the active option (RE20, RE21).
  • Server error on Send: "Not sent. NorthMES could not save the report. Your entries are kept. Press Send again. Error id 7f3a91c2." under Send, which names it with aria-describedby and keeps focus; the entries and the client_report_id stay, so the report counts once (RE24, RE25).
  • Field errors: the summary "2 fields need attention" at the top of the report card takes focus and links to Good quantity and to the Scrap reason group; "Enter whole pieces, for example 96." and "Choose a scrap reason. Scrap is above 0." sit next to their controls (RE26, OF19).

Units, ranges and WCAG 2.2 criteria

  • pcs: whole pieces, 0 or more. kg: one display decimal, typed with the decimal comma ("12,5"). A correction keeps the net per original at 0 or more.
  • 1.3.1 the dialogs, the fieldset and the keypad group; 2.1.1 every control by keyboard; 2.1.2 the modal dialogs release focus on their buttons and Escape; 2.4.3 focus into each dialog and back to its trigger; 2.4.11 the docked keypad never covers the focused field.
  • 2.5.8 targets of at least 44 px; 3.2.2 nothing is sent without Send or a dialog choice; 3.3.1 and 3.3.3 errors in text with the fix; 3.3.4 the confirmations and the correction row; 3.3.7 entries kept after an error; 4.1.2 aria-pressed, aria-disabled, aria-busy, aria-invalid, aria-selected; 4.1.3 "Sending" and the results as status messages.

Proposals and open questions

  • A correction counts as the job's last report in the meta line (RE16).
