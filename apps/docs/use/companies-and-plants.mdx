---
title: Companies and plants
description: How a NorthMES company and its plants relate, where the plant's and the company's settings live, and the host commands that create a company and recover its admin.
---

A company is the business that uses NorthMES. It owns its plants, its articles and article groups, its users and its roles. A plant is one production site of the company. One installation can hold several companies, and each user sees only the companies where they hold a role.

Roles are given at a place: at the company, where they apply at every plant of the company, also plants it gets later, or at one plant. See [Users, roles and permissions](/use/users-and-roles).

## Plant addresses

Each plant has a short name in its address, its slug, such as `plant-a` in `https://<NORTHMES_HOST>/plant-a`. The slug is unique in the installation. Every page at a plant sits below it, such as `/plant-a/articles`.

## Settings

The **Settings** button (the gear in the top bar) opens the settings of the plant you are at, on the first settings page you may open. Every settings page sits under the plant's settings, such as `/plant-a/settings/people`, and `/plant-a/settings` itself opens the first page you may open. On a settings page the sidebar collapses to its icons, and the settings navigation sits beside the page:

- The plant's settings come first, such as **People**, who holds a role at the plant.
- The navigation ends with a group named after your company, such as **Acme AB settings**, with the company's pages you may open: **Users** and **Roles**, and **Warehouses** for a user who manages the integrations, such as `/plant-a/settings/users`. These pages show the whole company, whichever of its plants you opened them from.

On **Your plants**, each company also has a settings link named after it, such as **Acme AB settings**, to the first of its pages you may open. The navigation lists only the pages you may open. A page you may not open says so and names the permission it needs.

Older addresses that start with `/settings/<company id>` lead to the same page under the settings of the company's first plant.

## Create a company

The hosting partner creates each company on the host with the `company create` command, as in [Install NorthMES](/install#install). The command creates the company with its default roles and its first Company admin. When it creates the admin's account, it prints a temporary password once; the admin signs in with it and chooses their own password. When the admin already has an account, the command gives that account the role and prints no password, so the admin signs in with their current one.

```sh
docker compose run --rm app company create --id <uuidv7> --name "Your company" \
  --admin-username admin --admin-email admin@example.com --reason "first install"
```

Give the company an id of your own with `--id`, a UUIDv7, and reuse it if you run the command again, for example after a failed run: a run with the same id finds the company the earlier run created and prints no new password. Without `--id`, every run creates another company.

To see the companies of the installation, with their ids, their number of plants and the usernames of their Company admins:

```sh
docker compose run --rm app company list
```

## When a company has no admin left

NorthMES refuses to remove the last Company admin of a company in the web app. If the company still loses every admin, for example because each admin left and was blocked, the hosting partner gives a user the Company admin role on the host:

```sh
docker compose run --rm app company add-admin --company <company id> \
  --username lena.ek --email lena.ek@example.com --reason "admin left"
```

A username that no user has creates the user with that email and prints a temporary password once. A blocked user is refused.

The hosting partner can also give a locked-out user a new temporary password. The user's sessions end, and the old password stops working. A blocked user is refused: unblock them first.

```sh
docker compose run --rm app admin reset-password --username lena.ek --reason "locked out"
```

Every command that changes data requires `--reason`. `company list` only reads and takes no flags.

## Limits in release 0.1.0

- Neither the web app nor the host commands can add a plant, so a company created with `company create` has no plants. The company's settings pages sit under its plants, so its admin can open neither a plant's pages nor the company's users and roles in the web app.
- The company's settings have no page to rename the company or to list its plants.
